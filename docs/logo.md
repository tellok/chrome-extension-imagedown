# ImageTaken Logo

使用内置 Imagegen 工具生成（不是 CLI/API fallback），原创黑白图片框与采集勾选符号。透明 PNG 原图保存在 [imagetaken-logo.png](../extension/assets/imagetaken-logo.png)，尺寸 1254 × 1254。工具栏 16 / 48 / 128 px 版本从同一原图等比缩小，保留透明度。标题栏使用 48 px 版本；README 使用原图。

完整生成提示词：

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create one original app logo symbol for ImageTaken, a browser tool collecting pictures from webpages. A bold elegant black ink image-frame icon with a small inward folded corner and a crisp check-shaped capture gesture integrated into the frame, one small sun dot and one mountain silhouette. Restrained editorial monochrome aesthetic matching a white paper document interface. Flat vector-like shapes, geometric optical balance, thick readable strokes for 16px favicon. Single centered symbol fills 80 percent of square canvas. Transparent background. Black and white only. No text, no letters, no gradients, no shadows, no mockup, no extra decorative elements, no existing brand logos.
```
